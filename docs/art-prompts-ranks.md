# Промты: эмблемы рангов Арены теней (10 штук)

Нужны для: окна Арены (рядом с названием ранга), Гардероба (рядом с титулом), окна «Новый ранг». Файлы `art/ui/rank-1.png … rank-10.png` (цвет камня = цвет ранга: красный … обсидиановый). Игра пока рисует ранги текстом; когда картинки появятся — подключу (хук `Art.has('ui/rank-N')`).

Партии по 5; во второй приложите первую удавшуюся эмблему как эталон стиля. Полный текст сообщений — на странице арт-промтов (партии «Эмблемы рангов Арены»).

Правила: квадрат 1024×1024, прозрачный фон, эмаль и металл, читается в ~56 px, без текста и цифр, набор одной семьи — к 10-му рангу рамка, крылья и корона богаче.

1. **rank-1** — Красный ранг: A simple round badge of dark oak wood with a thin iron rim, a single plain faceted ruby-red gem set in the center, a small crossed-blades mark scratched below it. Humble, newly made, no wings. Colors: warm brown wood, dull iron, a bright red gem.
2. **rank-2** — Оранжевый ранг: A round badge of bronze-toned metal with a narrow beaded rim, a faceted orange amber-colored gem in the center, two small plain leaf shapes at the sides. Colors: warm bronze, a glowing orange gem.
3. **rank-3** — Жёлтый ранг: A round badge of polished brass with a slightly wider rim, a faceted bright yellow citrine gem in the center, a short sword upright behind the gem. Colors: brass, a clear lemon-yellow gem.
4. **rank-4** — Зелёный ранг: A shield-shaped badge of dark steel with a green enamel border, a faceted emerald in the center, two small crossed swords behind it, a laurel sprig on each side. Colors: steel gray, forest green enamel, an emerald gem.
5. **rank-5** — Голубой ранг: A shield-shaped badge of bright steel with a light sky-blue enamel border, a faceted aquamarine gem in the center, crossed sword and mace behind it, a tiny pair of short wings at the top corners. Colors: steel, sky blue, a pale cyan gem.
6. **rank-6** — Синий ранг: A taller shield-shaped badge of dark silvered metal with a deep blue enamel border and fine engraved scrollwork, a large faceted sapphire in the center, crossed swords behind it, small folded wings at the sides. Colors: silver, royal blue, a deep sapphire gem.
7. **rank-7** — Фиолетовый ранг: A shield badge with a small crown-like top, deep purple enamel and silver filigree, a faceted amethyst in the center with a faint violet glow, crossed swords and a laurel wreath around it, short spread wings. Colors: silver, rich purple, an amethyst gem.
8. **rank-8** — Серебряный ранг: A grand shield badge fully made of bright polished silver with an engraved border, a faceted clear white-silver diamond-like gem in the center, a laurel wreath, crossed swords, spread wings, a small crown on top. Colors: bright silver, cool white highlights, pale blue shadows.
9. **rank-9** — Золотой ранг: A grand shield badge of shining gold with ornate engraved edges, a large faceted golden topaz in the center with a warm glow, a full laurel wreath, crossed swords, wide spread wings and a crown on top. Colors: rich gold, warm amber highlights, a deep red velvet inlay at the very bottom.
10. **rank-10** — Обсидиановый ранг: The most majestic badge: a shield of black volcanic obsidian with a thin glowing violet crack pattern and an edge of old silver, a large faceted black obsidian gem in the center with a violet inner glow, crossed swords, a laurel wreath of dark metal, wide spread wings and a crown on top, a few tiny violet sparks around it. Colors: glossy black, silver, violet glow.
